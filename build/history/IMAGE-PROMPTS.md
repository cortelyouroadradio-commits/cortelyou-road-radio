# Music History — image prompt pack

One ready-to-paste prompt per entry. Generate at **1600x900 (16:9)**, save as the
`save_as` filename, drop it in `assets/history/` and it appears automatically.

Works in ChatGPT Images 2.0, Google Imagen / Gemini, Midjourney (add `--ar 16:9`),
or Adobe Firefly. Full machine-readable list: `image-prompts.csv`.

> **On likenesses:** these prompts describe the room, the gear and the era rather than
> a named artist. That gets the same cinematic look without generating a real person's
> face, which is where the legal risk sits. Add a likeness yourself if you decide to.
