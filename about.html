About Cortelyou Road Radio

A local-first station rooted in Ditmas Park, Brooklyn — live radio, local stories, and Brooklyn events all in one daily check-in.

Cortelyou Road Radio is a daily soundtrack and a local signal at the same time — a place to hear the neighborhood and hear it reflected back with a little more shape.

Live music, neighborhood updates, and a short list of Brooklyn events, curated with a point of view instead of an endless scroll.
