Hip Hop Through the Years Part 1

A full hour of rap history, mixed in order of how it hit. Old school foundations roll forward into golden-era classics and the newer anthems that still move a room — East Coast and West Coast trading turns, big hooks, no dead air between them. Built for the drive, the workout, the party and the long night after.

About this mix
