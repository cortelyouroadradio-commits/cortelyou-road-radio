The Best Soft Rock Mix Ever

Fifty-eight minutes of the songs that never left the radio. Eagles, Elton John, Heart, Styx and REO Speedwagon set the 70s and 80s glow, then The Cranberries, Green Day, Red Hot Chili Peppers and Maroon 5 carry it through the 90s and 2000s. Four decades of soft rock hits, blended end to end, no dead air.

About this mix

The Best Soft Rock Mix Ever is a fifty-eight-minute soft rock DJ mix from Cortelyou Road Radio, mixed in Ditmas Park, Brooklyn. It runs 24 tracks nonstop, opening with Eagles's “Hotel California (Live at the Millennium Concert)” and closing on Maroon 5's “She Will Be Loved”, with no talking over the records.
