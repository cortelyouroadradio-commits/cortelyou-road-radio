About this mix

The Best 2000s R&B Mix is a fifty-seven-minute 2000s r&b / hip-hop DJ mix from Cortelyou Road Radio, mixed in Ditmas Park, Brooklyn. It runs 28 tracks nonstop, opening with Wale's “Lotus Flower Bomb (feat. Miguel)” and closing on Snoop Dogg's “Drop It Like It's Hot”, with no talking over the records.
