About this mix

An Hour of Funk is a forty-six-minute funk DJ mix from Cortelyou Road Radio, mixed in Ditmas Park, Brooklyn. It runs 18 tracks nonstop, opening with Zapp's “More Bounce to the Ounce” and closing on Kool & The Gang's “Hits Medley”, with no talking over the records.
