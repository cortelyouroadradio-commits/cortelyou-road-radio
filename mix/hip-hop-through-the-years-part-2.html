Hip Hop Through the Years Part 2

The story picks up where Part 1 left off. Fifty-five minutes running golden-era boom bap into the modern era — Big Daddy Kane, Slick Rick, De La Soul and House of Pain handing the baton to The Game, Kanye West and Wiz Khalifa. East Coast to West Coast, blended clean, no dead air.

About this mix

Hip Hop Through the Years Part 2 is a fifty-five-minute hip-hop DJ mix from Cortelyou Road Radio, mixed in Ditmas Park, Brooklyn. It runs 28 tracks nonstop, opening with Eric B. & Rakim's “Paid In Full” and closing on DJ Khaled's “All I Do Is Win”, with no talking over the records.
